HAMRO NOTES

Hamro Notes is a collaborative Android study app that brings notes, past questions, quizzes, syllabus tracking and entrance-preparation resources together for students in Nepal.

01 / ABOUT

About Hamro Notes

Hamro Notes helps students find course materials in one place. Its public app listing describes support for university notes and PDFs, previous question papers, subject quizzes, syllabus progress, study reminders and entrance-exam practice.

The app is designed for students across Nepal who want more organised access to study materials and preparation resources on Android.

02 / CONTRIBUTION

Shishir Acharya’s contribution

Hamro Notes is a collaborative student project. Shishir Acharya contributed to collecting and organising notes and learning resources as part of the project team alongside other students.

This attribution reflects a team contribution. It does not present Shishir as the founder, CEO or sole developer of Hamro Notes.

03 / FEATURES

Features for study and preparation

  • Subject-wise notes and PDFs
  • Past questions for semester and entrance examinations
  • Quizzes, self-assessment and progress history
  • Syllabus tracking, bookmarks and local study reminders
  • Preparation resources for BCA, CSIT and other programmes

04 / TECHNOLOGY

Technology and services

The public Google Play listing identifies Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics as services used to improve app stability and performance, and Google AdMob as supporting free access to content.

Specific implementation ownership and the complete application stack are not claimed on this portfolio page.

05 / DEVELOPMENT

A team-built learning product

Hamro Notes has grown from a student-led idea into a publicly available education app. The team continues to improve the learning experience and expand its study resources.
