HAMRO SIKSHYA

Hamro Sikshya is an education-focused web project intended to help students access blogs, select courses, join online classes and prepare for entrance exams through a clear online learning experience.

01 / ABOUT

About Hamro Sikshya

Hamro Sikshya is an education platform focused on making learning opportunities and resources easier to find online. Its purpose is to connect students with useful study information in a simple web experience.

The public site describes access to blogs, course selection, online classes, entrance-exam preparation and secure resource sharing.
